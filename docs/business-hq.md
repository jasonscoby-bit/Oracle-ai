# Business HQ — Operating Charter

_Last updated: 2026-10-29_

## Mission

Operate Rompiva and BullBear as two independent ventures under one practical AI-assisted operating system. The purpose is to turn focused work into measurable progress and, where commercially viable, revenue. This is an operating framework—not a promise of automated income.

## Operating principles

1. **Evidence before claims.** Never invent sales, audience, product, performance, accuracy, customer, or veterinary claims.
2. **Revenue before vanity metrics.** Prioritize qualified clicks, affiliate conversions, gross margin, and validated demand over likes and follower counts.
3. **Protect cash and credits.** Prefer existing tools, assets, and free workflows. Check cost/credit impact before paid generation or subscriptions.
4. **Small, reversible changes.** Back up or inspect before editing; test changes before publishing; do not overwrite working systems without a reason.
5. **Human approval at consequential boundaries.** No unapproved purchases, financial transactions, public posts, customer commitments, or changes to live accounts.
6. **Mobile-first execution.** Instructions and workflows should be practical from an iPhone. Avoid assuming access to a desktop computer.
7. **One clear next action.** Every work session ends with the highest-value next step, its owner, and what evidence will confirm completion.

## Venture B — BullBear / Oracle-AI

### Purpose
BullBear is the cryptocurrency market-direction and signal-performance component inside the broader Oracle-AI repository. It evaluates market data and measures whether its signals perform as intended. Oracle-AI's Polymarket trader-consensus work remains a distinct longer-term track.

### Operating guardrails
- Treat scores as research signals, not financial advice or guaranteed predictions.
- Do not describe a small sample as proven predictive accuracy.
- Report sample size, evaluation window, excluded/incomplete records, and methodology alongside every accuracy metric.
- Separate in-sample tuning from out-of-sample evaluation.
- Preserve raw signal history and avoid retroactively changing historical outcomes.
- Do not deploy score-weight changes solely to improve a backtest.

- Matured/closed signals versus pending signals
- Accuracy by horizon, asset, and score band
- Sample size and confidence intervals where appropriate
- Calibration: predicted confidence versus observed outcomes
- Data freshness, missing fields, duplicate records, and failed workflow runs
- Regression-test and workflow pass rates

## Shared AI operator workflow

For each work session:

1. **Inspect:** Read the current source of truth (repo, workflow, project, live page, or supplied evidence).
2. **Diagnose:** Separate confirmed facts, assumptions, and unknowns.
3. **Prioritize:** Select the highest expected-value action, accounting for risk, time, cash, and credits.
4. **Execute:** Make the smallest safe change possible.
5. **Verify:** Re-read the result, run relevant tests, inspect the mobile/live output, or request the missing evidence.
6. **Report:** Summarize what changed, what passed, what remains unverified, and the next action.

## Decision rubric

Score proposed work from 1–5 on:
- Revenue or decision value
- Evidence strength
- Cost efficiency
- Time-to-feedback
- Reversibility / risk (higher score means safer)

Prioritize evidence-backed, low-cost, reversible work with a short feedback loop. Do not use a numerical score to disguise uncertainty.

## First implementation phases

### Phase 1 — Establish reliable baselines
- Rompiva: inspect the latest Creatify project, identify the intended approved take, complete playback/claim/CTA QA, and verify the live affiliate path on mobile.
- BullBear: inspect the latest workflow runs and current signal data; rerun only relevant QA; publish a concise baseline report with sample sizes and limitations.
- Create one shared decision log and weekly review format.

### Phase 2 — Build repeatable operating routines
- Rompiva: weekly product shortlist, link audit, content brief, and conversion review.
- BullBear: scheduled data-quality checks, matured-signal performance review, calibration review, and regression tests.
- Keep automation narrow and avoid duplicate notifications.

### Phase 3 — Add integrations only when justified
Connect publishing, analytics, customer messaging, or sales systems only when the integration is supported, permissions are clear, and the expected benefit exceeds setup and recurring cost.

## Approval boundaries

The AI operator may research, analyze, draft, prepare code changes, and run non-destructive checks within connected permissions. Ask for explicit approval before spending money or credits on a new paid asset, publishing public content, sending customer messages, changing live business settings, making financial transactions, or deploying a material strategy/code change.

## Weekly review template

- **Rompiva:** What shipped? What traffic/conversions/commission were measured? Which links or claims need repair? What is the next low-cost experiment?
- **BullBear:** What new signals matured? What is the sample size? What changed by score band and asset? Did tests/workflows pass? What remains uncertain?
- **Shared:** What produced measurable value? What consumed time/credits without value? What one task should happen next?
