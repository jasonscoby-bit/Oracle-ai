# Oracle-AI

## Overview

Oracle-AI is an open-source analytics platform that identifies high-conviction opportunities on Polymarket by analyzing the behavior of elite traders.

Instead of following a single whale, Oracle-AI measures consensus among the top active traders and transforms that data into actionable intelligence.

---

## Mission

Use quantitative analysis to remove emotion from prediction market investing.

Oracle-AI identifies where the world's best Polymarket traders independently agree and converts that information into a simple confidence score.

---

## Current Objectives

- Track the Top 100 active Polymarket traders
- Analyze trader specialties
- Detect elite trader consensus
- Measure conviction using position size
- Calculate an Oracle Score for every market
- Generate a Heat Index highlighting the strongest opportunities

---

## Oracle Score

Every market will eventually receive a score from 0–100 based on factors such as:

- Active trader consensus
- Combined capital committed
- Historical trader performance
- Position conviction
- Entry timing
- Market category

Higher scores indicate stronger agreement among elite traders.

---

## Heat Index

🟢 Cold – Minimal elite participation

🟡 Warm – Moderate whale activity

🟠 Hot – Strong consensus forming

🔴 Oracle Alert – Multiple elite traders independently building high-conviction positions
